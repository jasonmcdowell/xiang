Xiang character indices

Source: Make Me a Hanzi, https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi
The dictionary is derived from Unihan and CJKlib and distributed under GNU LGPL v3 or later. See licenses/makemeahanzi-COPYING.txt and licenses/LGPL.txt (and its incorporated terms in licenses/GPL-3.0.txt).

Xiang's modifications: scripts/build_indices.js streams dictionary.txt, retains complete supported immediate decompositions and validates explicit nested recipes from data/decomposition_extensions.json, normalizes six common component variants, builds an order-insensitive reverse index, counts components, and extracts pinyin and definitions. Source records are not manually edited. Generation can be repeated using the upstream dictionary and the script in this repository. The raw dictionary and graphics are intentionally not included in this repository.

Generated files: decomp.json, compose_pairs.json, component_freq.json, meta.json.

Physical playground: playground/xiang.json contains the 13 stroke outlines of 想 extracted from graphics.txt by scripts/build-playground.mjs (2026-09-22). This derived graphics asset is distributed under the Arphic Public License; see licenses/ARPHICPL.TXT. Copyright (C) 1999 Arphic Technology Co., Ltd. The asset records its modifications and source; it is freely redistributable under that license, without warranty. The raw graphics dataset remains local-only.

Experimental HSK-derived character lists and pools are generated from Complete HSK Vocabulary by drkameleon, https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary. The source is distributed under the MIT License; a copy is in licenses/HSK-MIT.txt. Xiang's lists and pools are filtered/generated derivatives.

The experimental idiom list and scramble file are derived from ChID-Dataset by Chujie Zheng, Minlie Huang, and Aixin Sun, https://github.com/chujiezheng/ChID-Dataset. See the ACL 2019 paper “ChID: A Large-scale Chinese IDiom Dataset for Cloze Test.” The source repository is licensed under Apache License 2.0; a copy is in licenses/ChID-APACHE-2.0.txt. Xiang's scramble output is generated by scripts/build_chid_scrambles.js.

These HSK and ChID experimental assets are not loaded by the current game.
